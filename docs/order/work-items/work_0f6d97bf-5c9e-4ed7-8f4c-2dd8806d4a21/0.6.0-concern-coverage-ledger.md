## 1. Coverage method

G1 proves the campaign register against four independent factual lanes:

1. every stable P2/P3 product/functional requirement ID;
2. every canonical P2/P3 owner, including normative sections without GW-FR ids;
3. every user-visible/product workflow surface and lifecycle stage;
4. every productive project-governance/provider/settings boundary.

The ledger maps authority to campaign concerns; it does not restate or modify the underlying normative semantics.

The ledger is an authority-coverage map only. It must not inventory current application behavior, current UI/design state, implementation quirks, compatibility behavior or preservation targets. Product surfaces appear here only to prove that each governed concern has campaign coverage; their present implementation is not a design baseline.

Its purpose is to support reconstruction of the complete intended functional design that the 0.5.0 work was supposed to realize from canonical P2/P3 authority. It does not prove or preserve the released 0.5.0 implementation, and no implementation or browser regression may alter concern coverage unless it exposes missing or contradictory canonical authority.
