## 1. Purpose

This document governs the complete Order 0.6.0 design campaign from prerequisite investigation through an adversarially reviewable implementation plan, mandatory vertical functional/technical/implementation alignment closure and final R3 readiness review.

The campaign is intentionally concern-driven rather than screen-driven or implementation-driven. Its primary objective is to reconstruct, from canonical P2/P3 authority, the complete intended functional design that the 0.5.0 work was supposed to realize; then derive the 0.6.0 technical solution design and implementation plan that will actually realize that design. The released 0.5.0 application, its tests and its rendered UI are evidence only and never define what 0.6.0 must preserve. The campaign makes omissions visible by ensuring that every material product concern has an explicit investigation start, an explicit factual end, a functional-design disposition, a technical-design disposition and final implementation-plan coverage.

## 4. No current-state fallback / no regression-baseline rule

The 0.6.0 campaign is an authority-driven design campaign. **Current application state, current UI composition, current implementation behavior and current design shape are not campaign outputs and are never a fallback definition of desired behavior.**

Current code, tests, rendered UI, historical design documents and prior releases may be inspected only as transient evidence to:

- prove a defect or contradiction against canonical authority;
- identify an existing capability that a later DESIGN decision may deliberately reuse;
- locate an ownership/boundary failure;
- determine migration/deletion consequences after the required future behavior is already established;
- prove that a claimed correction actually landed.

They must **not** be copied into this plan, `research.md`, `requirements.md`, `solution-design.md` or another campaign artifact as a descriptive inventory of "how the application currently works", a preservation baseline, compatibility target, regression contract, fallback behavior or implicit requirement.

The governing direction is always:

```text
canonical product intent / authority
        -> factual gap or unresolved decision
        -> required 0.6.0 behavior
        -> technical realization
        -> implementation plan
```

and never:

```text
current application/design state
        -> preserve by default
        -> document as desired behavior
```

A concern investigation therefore records only:

1. applicable canonical authority and accepted intent;
2. contradictions, missing decisions, ownership defects or unproven obligations;
3. exact decision inputs required by R2/DESIGN;
4. cross-concern dependencies;
5. verification evidence sufficient to support the finding.

Implementation observations remain cited evidence behind a finding; they are not restated as product facts unless the same statement is independently owned by canonical authority. Absence of a finding does not mean "keep the current application"; it means the existing canonical authority is sufficient for that concern.

Any wording such as "preserve current", "keep existing", "match current", "unchanged behavior", "current design is correct", or equivalent is invalid unless it refers to an explicitly cited canonical semantic obligation rather than incidental implementation/design state.

### 4.1 Campaign-purpose precedence

The purpose of this campaign outranks transient evidence and delivery status. A PR/CI result, browser test, existing regression, current implementation behavior, historical design statement, concern status note or handover may not become a functional requirement, preservation contract or campaign gate by implication. Such evidence may stop the campaign only when it proves that the canonical authority, document-authoring contract or conformance machinery needed to reconstruct R1/R2/DESIGN/PLAN is itself defective. Otherwise it is routed to the appropriate later design, implementation or delivery work and the authority-driven reconstruction continues.

Browser/UI tests become relevant to 0.6.0 acceptance only after reconstructed functional requirements and technical design establish the behavior they must verify. Existing browser tests do not constrain the reconstructed design merely because they describe or protect 0.5.0 behavior.

### 4.2 Exhaustive semantic and realization proof

The prior campaign pass established broad authority/concern coverage but did not prove the complete semantic content and realization of every applicable obligation. That failure invalidates the former G2-G6 closure and requires R1 revalidation before downstream lifecycle artifacts can be trusted again.

The campaign must distinguish three independent completeness proofs:

1. **Authority coverage** — every applicable canonical owner, stable requirement and normative section has been examined.
2. **Semantic/information coverage** — every material obligation has been atomized into its required information, hierarchy, work-kind/stage variation, interaction, state, transition, failure, recovery, negative behavior and acceptance consequence.
3. **Realization coverage** — every obligation has a complete producer -> carrier -> consumer path through the actual authoring/schema/prompt/state/contract/runtime/browser/presentation boundaries that apply to it.

For every material obligation, current realization evidence is classified only as `CONFORMS`, `VIOLATES` or `UNPROVEN`. `UNPROVEN` prevents factual closure.

Concern mapping and stable-ID mapping are necessary inventory controls only. They are not semantic-conformance proof. Likewise, `REFERENCE`, `no functional delta`, `no technical change`, `no implementation change` and `already satisfied` are evidence-backed verdicts, never defaults.

Where the canonical authority defines screen- or stage-specific information design, the audit must be screen/stage specific. At minimum the Workbench proof covers every applicable combination of workflow step, work kind, interaction condition, current-attention condition, material/currentness condition, current/historical selection and responsive class. The audit must prove the information producer, canonical carrier, presentation derivation, rendered hierarchy and valid action for each material value.

## 8. Concern investigation protocol

Every concern uses the same explicit START -> investigation -> END protocol.

### 8.1 START record

Before evidence collection for a concern begins, add a START record to the concern log in this document:

```text
- date:
- current phase: R1

Canonical owners:
- ...

Requirement families:
- ...

Upstream intent:

1. What does canonical authority require for this concern?
2. Is that authority complete and internally consistent for 0.6.0?
3. What exact gaps or decisions remain before future behavior is unambiguous?
4. Which adjacent concerns can invalidate this concern's closure?
```

A concern is then changed from `NOT_STARTED` to `IN_REVIEW`.

### 8.2 Mandatory R1 analysis dimensions

Each concern investigation must close:

1. **Intent** — canonical P2/product reason and required user outcome.
2. **Authority** — canonical owner(s), stable requirement identities and applicable higher rules.
3. **Authority completeness** — contradictions, missing decisions, ambiguous ownership or unproven obligations that prevent a complete 0.6.0 decision.
4. **Information ownership** — required canonical source facts and semantic producer -> carrier -> consumer ownership where the authority depends on them.
5. **Interaction obligations** — only the operator-visible behavior required or left undecided by canonical authority.
6. **State/transition closure** — required lifecycle/material/proposal/approval/task/provider semantics and any missing decision.
7. **Negative behavior** — prohibited behavior and failure semantics owned by authority.
8. **Recovery obligations** — blocker, retry, stale, cancellation and return-to-owner requirements.
9. **Cross-concern interfaces** — authority/decision dependencies imported from or exported to other Cxx concerns.
10. **Proof state** — obligation-level evidence sufficient to classify every material current realization as `CONFORMS`, `VIOLATES` or `UNPROVEN`, without turning observed implementation/design state into a campaign-owned baseline.
12. **Authoring/input/output path** — schema/template/prompt/context inputs, generated outputs and structural/semantic conformance needed to preserve the obligation precisely.
13. **Realization path** — complete producer -> carrier -> consumer flow through canonical contracts/state, core/daemon/shell, persistence/transport, browser projection and rendered/operator consumption where applicable.
14. **Information design** — exact primary, secondary and technical/on-demand information, including work-kind/stage distinctions and current-attention overrides wherever user-visible.
15. **Verification ownership** — the permanent sensor or semantic review needed to prove the obligation rather than merely the existence of its identifier.

Current code, tests, UI and prior design may be inspected to prove a finding, but their incidental shape is never recorded as desired behavior, preservation scope or fallback semantics.

`VERIFIED` means the factual model is closed; it does **not** mean no 0.6.0 functional change is needed.

No concern reaches R2 while it has an unresolved factual question that could materially change the functional decision.

#### Pair-audit progression law

For a given phase:

1. the two concerns in the current pair are completed under the normal phase rules;
2. the pair audit enters `IN_REVIEW`;
3. the auditor reopens the exact current artifacts/evidence for both concerns and performs every applicable general and phase-specific check below;
4. the audit returns `PASS` or `FAIL`;
5. `PASS` authorizes the next pair at that phase;
6. `FAIL` reopens the defective concern(s) at the exact owning phase and the same pair must be re-audited after correction;
7. a missing audit is progression-equivalent to `FAIL`.

No model, handover, status summary or later lifecycle artifact may claim progress in pair A(n+1) while pair An lacks a current `PASS` at that same phase.

The audit must be adversarial. It may reuse prior evidence, but it must not trust the two concerns' own completion assertions.

#### General pair-audit checklist

Every pair audit must prove, for **each** of its two concerns:

1. all applicable canonical owners and normative clauses were actually inspected;
2. every material obligation was atomized rather than hidden behind a requirement ID, concern ID or broad summary;
3. every mandatory phase dimension was answered with evidence;
4. evidence is current for the exact reviewed repository/document baseline;
5. implementation/UI/tests/history were used only as evidence and never as desired-state authority;
6. every producer -> carrier -> consumer interface required by the concern is accounted for;
7. every applicable state, transition, interaction, failure, recovery and negative behavior is explicit;
8. work-kind and workflow-stage variants are explicit where applicable;
9. every cross-concern dependency/finding has an owner and is not silently deferred;
10. reused prior material is classified and independently justified rather than inherited by status;
11. there is no duplicate/shadow canonical owner created by the concern artifact;
12. no material item is left `UNPROVEN`.

The audit also challenges the **interface between the two concerns**: assumptions exported by one must agree with facts/requirements/owners consumed by the other.

## 9. Finding and dependency discipline

### 9.1 Stable finding identities

Use:

- `Cxx-Fnn` — finding owned by one concern;
- `X-Cxx-Cyy-nn` — finding discovered in Cxx but requiring disposition in Cyy;
- `G-Fnn` — genuinely campaign-wide foundational finding;
- `ALIGN-Axx-Fnn` — finding discovered by a vertical ALIGN audit. This is a campaign-local trace identity only: the finding must be routed to the earliest lifecycle/canonical owner that can correct it and must never become a new semantic authority owner.

A finding is recorded once at its primary owner and referenced elsewhere. ALIGN findings remain open until every required upstream/foundation/PLAN correction is complete and the affected ALIGN audit is freshly rerun; correcting an upstream document alone does not resolve an ALIGN finding whose executable baseline or downstream package is still stale.

For current A06 findings, `OPEN_FOUNDATION` means the governing authority correction is documented but the required execution-baseline capability has not yet been implemented, integrated by ancestry and independently verified; it therefore keeps G0 in revalidation. `OPEN_PLAN` means no further upstream/foundation semantic correction is required, but the final regenerated PLAN still must incorporate the owned correction before the finding can resolve.

### 9.2 Cross-concern rule

When C06 exposes a problem in C11, C06 records `X-C06-C11-nn`; C06 does not redesign Approval while still investigating Research.

The C11 START record must list that incoming finding. C11 cannot close until it has explicitly disposed it.

### 9.3 Foundational defect rule

If an investigation exposes a Phase 0-class problem—ambiguous canonical ownership, broken reference lineage, shadow authority, impossible lifecycle topology, or another foundation defect—the active concern becomes `BLOCKED`.

The defect is remediated at its canonical owner through a separately governed WorkItem. After that remediation lands, all concerns whose evidence/authority depended on the changed foundation enter `REVALIDATION`.

## 13A. Phase 3A — ALIGN final vertical domain completion

ALIGN is the campaign's **mandatory final content-completion stage**. It begins only after G5 has established a complete PLAN candidate and it completes before R3 may begin.

ALIGN does not create product, technical or implementation authority. It treats the exact current R2 `requirements.md`, DESIGN `solution-design.md` and PLAN `implementation-plan.md` as untrusted inputs and proves that the same obligations are complete, semantically consistent and executable **vertically through all three layers**. R1 and current implementation/UI/tests/history may be consulted only as evidence for challenging those mappings; they do not define the desired 0.6.0 baseline.

The purpose of ALIGN is to prevent layer-local PASS results from hiding cross-layer omissions, semantic drift, delegated architecture decisions, unowned verification obligations or an implementation sequence that cannot actually execute the designed solution.

### 13A.1 Entry contract

ALIGN may start only when:

- G3, G4 and G5 are `PASS`;
- the exact current R2, DESIGN and PLAN Git blob identities are recorded in the `ALIGN-A01` baseline;
- all required PLAN source-component hashes resolve to the bytes compiled into the root PLAN;
- no unresolved campaign finding already invalidates R2, DESIGN or PLAN;
- the auditor is evaluating the package rather than repairing it in place.

A mutation to R2, DESIGN, PLAN, a permanent authority owner materially consumed by them, or a structural fitness rule that changes the meaning of the package invalidates every affected ALIGN audit and G5A until the exact corrected package is re-audited.

### 13A.2 Domain sequence

ALIGN uses the campaign's existing dependency-ordered A01-A11 domain grouping. It does not invent a second taxonomy.

| Alignment audit | Concerns | Vertical domain |
|---|---|---|
| `ALIGN-A01` | C01 + C21 | lifecycle, product model, outcome and product language |
| `ALIGN-A02` | C22 + C02 | project authority/governance and WorkItem intake |
| `ALIGN-A03` | C03 + C04 | queue, source admission and repository/document Git |
| `ALIGN-A04` | C05 + C06 | Workbench/current attention and Research/human input |
| `ALIGN-A05` | C07 + C08 | Requirements and DESIGN authority mutation |
| `ALIGN-A06` | C09 + C10 | PLAN contract and R3/readiness |
| `ALIGN-A07` | C11 + C12 | Approval, execution, checkpoints and validation |
| `ALIGN-A08` | C13 + C14 | terminal completion and governed materials |
| `ALIGN-A09` | C15 + C16 | provider integration, installation and Settings |
| `ALIGN-A10` | C17 + C18 | recovery/current attention and accessibility/focus |
| `ALIGN-A11` | C19 + C20 | component/theme system and end-to-end certification |

The sequence is mandatory. `ALIGN-A(n+1)` may not start until `ALIGN-An` is `PASS`. Reordering is permitted only when newly proven dependency evidence requires it; the dependency and revised order must be recorded here before the affected audit begins.

### 13A.3 Mandatory vertical trace

For every material atomic obligation in the domain, the audit must prove the complete chain:

```text
canonical functional authority
  -> exact R2 required behavior / state / transition / failure / recovery / acceptance
  -> exact DESIGN realization or evidence-backed positive-no-change disposition
  -> canonical semantic owner and producer -> carrier -> consumer / effect path
  -> exact PLAN IA action or evidence-backed no-code action
  -> executable dependency/order/checkpoint
  -> exact permanent proof proposition / test / sensor / gate
```

The audit must also prove the reverse direction:

```text
every DESIGN decision -> accepted functional obligation
every PLAN action -> accepted DESIGN/functional obligation
every verification mutation -> exact behavior/architecture proposition it proves
```

Shared technical solutions or implementation actions may satisfy multiple functional obligations only when every relationship is explicit and the shared owner is semantically correct. Crossover is not a defect; **implicit crossover is**.

Counts, matching IDs, hashes, green gates or presence of an SD/IA identifier are necessary structural evidence but are never sufficient semantic proof.

### 13A.3.1 Mandatory ALIGN quality standard

Every ALIGN audit applies the same non-negotiable quality bar. The purpose is not to demonstrate that artifacts are broadly consistent; it is to prove that **nothing material is omitted, compressed beyond safe traceability, delegated implicitly, or left for implementation inference**.

For each atomic obligation, the auditor records every applicable quality dimension below as `PROVEN`. `PARTIAL`, `MISSING`, `INFERRED`, `STALE` or `NOT_CHECKED` is a domain `FAIL`, not a qualified pass.

| Quality ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Q1 — Authority completeness | Every applicable stable ID **and normative clause without a stable ID**, P2/P3 correction, package-local consequence and unresolved/imported dependency is represented. Identifier cardinality cannot substitute for clause-level coverage. |
| Q2 — Functional explicitness | R2 itself states the complete applicable work-kind/stage/information contract: observable behavior, displayed/entered/generated/reviewed information, hierarchy, interaction, state/action/transition, blocker/failure/recovery/return/terminal semantics, acceptance, exclusions and downstream consequence. A REFERENCE/stable-ID row, concern summary or certification matrix cannot substitute for the functional description. |
| Q3 — Technical completeness | DESIGN itself resolves the exact canonical semantic owner, shared-versus-kind/stage-specific boundary, schema/template/context/prompt/provider/session consequences, document/event/state carrier, pure/effect ownership, Workbench composition, producer→carrier→consumer/data-control path, reuse/removal/replacement decision, required decomposition, downstream invariants, failure/recovery behavior, proof owner and material rejected alternatives or explicit N/A rationale. POSITIVE_NO_CHANGE/DESIGN-positive reuse/certification alone cannot substitute for this realization. |
| Q4 — Positive-no-change proof | A no-change conclusion is requirement-specific and positively proves the current canonical realization against the exact R1 evidence. “Existing behavior”, green tests, concern closure or a generic preservation statement is insufficient. |
| Q5 — Corrected-upstream retention | An obligation already corrected in P2/P3/P4-P6 remains explicitly represented downstream as an evidence-backed `NO_CODE / ALREADY_CORRECTED` disposition. It must not disappear merely because no T1 edit remains. |
| Q6 — Implementation exactness | Every technical delta has one exact IA execution owner with concrete mutation, file/module/symbol responsibility, forbidden alternatives, dependencies and completion evidence. Every IA action traces upward to accepted functional/design authority. |
| Q7 — Executability | Ordered actions and architecture-feedback checkpoints are executable from the actual signed baseline/runtime. No bootstrap, self-hosting, permission or sequencing assumption is implicit. |
| Q8 — Verification proposition closure | Every changed or positively preserved obligation identifies the **exact proposition** to prove, exact test/sensor owner, required mutation/no-mutation, and gate. Generic “add regression coverage”, “semantic review”, “tests pass” or later gap-filling is insufficient for a known proposition. |
| Q9 — Cross-domain interface closure | Every exported/imported producer, carrier, state, authority, outcome, proof or execution dependency agrees with its consuming domain and has a named owner. |
| Q10 — Minimum-change / architecture integrity | The package neither preserves a defective current shape nor forces new responsibility into a fixed file list. Architecture-preserving same-layer decomposition remains permitted exactly as higher authority allows; semantic architecture changes return upstream. |
| Q11 — Freshness and identity | Claims bind to the exact current authority/R1/R2/DESIGN/PLAN bytes and current repository evidence. Any material mutation invalidates affected conclusions transitively. |
| Q12 — Zero residual inference | After R1 + R2 + DESIGN + PLAN are read, an implementation worker has **zero material product, information-design, work-kind/stage-specialization, OpenCode-integration, UI-composition, architecture, ownership, contract, sequencing or verification decision left to reconstruct or make**. Required meaning may be referenced to its canonical owner, but the package mapping may not depend on reading current implementation/tests to discover the design. |

Additional quality laws:

- **No compression loss:** a grouped row may be used only when every atomic obligation within it has the same owner, realization, execution disposition and proof. Otherwise it must be decomposed.
- **No orphaned correction:** corrected authority, deleted behavior and explicit no-code outcomes remain traceable through the matrix even when they produce no T1 mutation.
- **No proof by adjacency:** a nearby test, shared gate or broader certification row is not proof unless the exact proposition is stated and owned.
- **No “eventual closure” substitution:** a generic final regression/closeout action cannot own a proof obligation that is already known during DESIGN/PLAN.
- **No aggregate PASS override:** one failed atomic row or one failed quality dimension fails the domain.
- **No downstream progress after FAIL:** the earliest defective owner is reopened immediately; the next ALIGN domain remains blocked until the corrected package is freshly re-audited and the current domain passes.

The audit record must include a quality summary showing Q1-Q12 as `PROVEN` or the exact failed dimension/finding ID. This quality section is normative for every `ALIGN-Axx` and `ALIGN-H1` audit.

### 13A.4 Per-domain audit requirements

Each `ALIGN-Axx` audit must adversarially prove all of the following:

1. **Functional completeness** — every material authority/R1 obligation has an explicit R2 behavior, state/action/transition, failure/recovery/negative behavior, acceptance and exclusion consequence where applicable.
2. **Functional -> technical completeness** — every R2 obligation has an exact DESIGN realization, including canonical owner, affected boundaries, data/control path, persistence/transport/provider consequences and required failure/recovery semantics.
3. **Technical -> implementation completeness** — every DESIGN obligation maps to an exact PLAN action or positively proven no-code disposition; known DESIGN work must not be delegated to a generic later gap-filling action.
4. **Implementation -> authority legitimacy** — every IA action maps upward to accepted DESIGN and functional authority; no orphan action or speculative implementation is permitted.
5. **Ownership integrity** — one canonical semantic owner is preserved; no DTO, shadow model, duplicate persistence/transport/UI policy or browser-owned business policy is introduced.
6. **Executable ordering** — dependencies, internal task ordering and architecture-feedback checkpoints can actually execute on the runtime/baseline that performs the signed task; no self-hosting or bootstrap assumption is left implicit.
7. **Change-surface integrity** — task/change-surface constraints do not force duplicate responsibility, prevent authority-permitted architecture-preserving decomposition or hide an unsigned architecture decision.
8. **Verification completeness** — every functional acceptance and technical invariant has an exact proof owner and PLAN verification action where mutation is required; generic final regression closure may not substitute for a known proof obligation.
9. **Positive-no-change validity** — every no-functional/no-technical/no-code conclusion has requirement-specific positive evidence for the complete obligation and its proof owner; current implementation behavior alone is never authority.
10. **Cross-domain interfaces** — every producer/carrier/consumer or state/dependency exported to another Axx domain agrees with what that domain consumes.
11. **Minimum sufficient change** — no planned mutation exists merely because the current implementation has it, and no required correction is omitted merely to preserve an earlier file list, test or implementation shape.
12. **Zero material inference** — an implementation worker must not decide product behavior, semantic ownership, architecture, task contract, verification semantics or between material solution alternatives.

Any single failed item makes the domain audit `FAIL`.

Every `ALIGN-Axx` record must use the exact current repository/artifact baseline and include a row for every material atomic obligation:

- missing functional obligations;
- R2 obligations without complete technical realization;
- DESIGN decisions without upstream functional authority;

### 13A.6 Failure, correction and staleness law

ALIGN is an audit stage, not a repair stage.

When `ALIGN-Axx` finds a defect:

1. record the finding and identify the **earliest defective owner**;
2. return to R1, R2, DESIGN or PLAN as required by that ownership;
3. invalidate every downstream artifact/conclusion whose inputs changed;
4. keep the failed ALIGN record as historical evidence only;
5. regenerate/revalidate the affected package under the ordinary lifecycle gates;
6. restart the affected ALIGN audit on the exact new R2/DESIGN/PLAN blobs;
7. re-run every later ALIGN audit whose assumptions or evidence transitively depended on the changed material.

A later domain may not be audited against a package containing an earlier failed domain. Findings may not be accumulated while progression continues.

A correction must be made in the existing canonical owner. ALIGN must not create a shadow specification, technical design, task plan or verification model to make the audit pass.
